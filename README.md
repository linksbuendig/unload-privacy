# Privacy Policy — UnLoad

_Last updated: 2026-04-19_

This Privacy Policy describes how the UnLoad mobile application ("UnLoad", "the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and stores your personal data. By creating an account and using UnLoad, you agree to the practices described below.

## 1. Who we are

UnLoad is a shared household app that lets you manage shopping lists, recipes, and meal plans together with the members of your household.

- Developer: linksbuendig

## 2. Data we collect

We collect and store only the data that is needed to operate the app.

### 2.1 Account data
- Email address
- Password (stored in hashed form by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store the plain password)
- Display name

### 2.2 Household and content data
- Households you create or belong to, including the household name and the list of members
- Shopping list items (name, quantity, unit, completion state)
- Recipes you create or import (title, ingredients, instructions, images)
- Meal plan entries (date, meal type, recipe or free text)
- Invitations you send or receive

### 2.3 Recipe import data
When you import a recipe from a URL, clipboard text, or photo, the content is sent to our import service to be parsed into a structured recipe. This content is processed to extract the recipe and is stored in your account together with the imported recipe.

### 2.4 Technical data
- Crash and error logs generated by Firebase, used to diagnose problems
- Basic device information necessary to run the app
- Offline cache on your device so the app works without an internet connection

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

## 3. How we use your data

We use the data listed above to:

- Authenticate you and keep your session
- Sync your households, shopping lists, recipes, and meal plans between your devices and other household members
- Deliver invitations to the email addresses you provide
- Import recipes that you submit from URLs, text, or images
- Diagnose crashes and fix bugs

We do not use your content for profiling or advertising.

## 4. Who can see your data

- **You**: all data in your account
- **Members of your households**: the content of households you share with them (shopping list, recipes in that household, meal plan, member list)
- **People you invite**: their email address is used only to deliver the invitation
- **Service providers we rely on** (see Section 6), strictly to operate the app
- **No one else.** We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or analytics purposes.

## 5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored by Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage) on Google's infrastructure. Depending on Firebase's regional configuration, data may be processed in data centers inside and outside the European Union. Google provides appropriate safeguards for international transfers (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

## 6. Service providers

We use the following third-party processors:

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Crashlytics if enabled) — backend, storage, authentication, crash reporting
- **Recipe import provider** — if you use the recipe import feature, the submitted URL, text, or image is sent to an AI-based parsing service to extract the structured recipe

These providers process your data on our behalf and under a data processing agreement.

## 7. Retention

- Account and household data are kept for as long as your account exists.
- When you delete a household you own, that household and its contents are removed.
- When you delete your account, your user profile, the households you own, and your membership in other households are removed. Content you contributed to households owned by other users (e.g. shopping list items, recipes) may remain in those households, since the household owner controls them.
- Crash logs are retained according to Firebase defaults (typically up to 90 days).

## 8. Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA, the GDPR gives you the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Export your data in a portable format
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Withdraw consent at any time
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

You can edit your profile and delete your account from the Settings screen in the app.

## 9. Children

UnLoad is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable age). Do not use the app if you are below that age.

## 10. Security

We rely on Firebase's security features (encrypted transport, managed authentication, Firestore security rules) to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure — use a strong, unique password and keep your device protected.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates the latest version.

## 12. Contact

Please contact the developer via the Play Store.
